<Blockquote author="Jeremy Laird" source="PC Gamer" sourceUrl="https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/memory/many-consumer-electronics-manufacturers-will-go-bankrupt-or-exit-product-lines-by-the-end-of-2026-due-to-the-ai-memory-crisis-phison-ceo-reportedly-says/">Is it just me, or does it feel like everything is just about to spiral out of control with this AI-memory-boom-chatbot-slop-crisis thing? ## Mount Mayhem at Netflix: Scaling Containers on Modern CPUs

<Blockquote author="Harshad Sane, Andrew Halaney" source="Netflix Technology Blog" sourceUrl="https://netflixtechblog.com/mount-mayhem-at-netflix-scaling-containers-on-modern-cpus-f3b09b68beac">Imagine this — you click play on Netflix on a Friday night and behind the scenes hundreds of containers spring to action in a few seconds to answer your call. At Netflix, scaling containers efficiently is critical to delivering a seamless streaming experience to millions of members worldwide. To keep up with responsiveness at this scale, we modernized our container runtime, only to hit a surprising bottleneck: the CPU architecture itself.</Blockquote>

## minimatch Patches 3 High-Severity ReDoS Vulnerabilities

<Blockquote author="Sarah Gooding" source="Socket" sourceUrl="https://socket.dev/blog/minimatch-patches-3-high-severity-redos-vulnerabilities">On February 24, GitHub published two High severity security advisories for the widely used npm package minimatch. A third High severity advisory was published earlier on February 16.</Blockquote>

<Blockquote author="Sarah Gooding" source="Socket" sourceUrl="https://socket.dev/blog/minimatch-patches-3-high-severity-redos-vulnerabilities">All three vulnerabilities can lead to Regular Expression Denial of Service \(ReDoS\) and event loop starvation in Node.js when malicious glob patterns are processed.</Blockquote>
